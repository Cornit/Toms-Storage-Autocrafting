Autocrafting Terminal

Runs the crafting jobs of the network; at least one is needed. It lists all craftable items and all jobs on the network.

- Select an item, enter an amount and request it.
- Each job shows its progress; hover for the steps and why one waits (missing items, busy provider).
- Buttons per job: priority (high/normal/low), pause, cancel. When a job is cancelled, inputs still inside machines can be returned (config returnInputsOnCancel).
- Tabs Running/History: the history lists the last finished or cancelled jobs (✕ clears it), running jobs show the estimated time left in their tooltip.
- Jobs reserve their materials, so two jobs never use the same items.
- Capacity Upgrades allow more parallel jobs.
