Requesting items

Craftable items show a green + in every Tom's terminal on the network.

- Ctrl + click a craftable item, or click one with amount 0, to open the request dialog.
- Enter the amount and press Calculate: you get a material list (from storage, to craft, missing); the Tree button opens the recipe tree as a graph: drag to move, mouse wheel to zoom, click a node to expand or collapse it; red frame = missing, blue = crafted, grey = from storage. You can start the job from there too.
- Start begins the job. If something is missing, a button offers the largest amount that can be crafted now.
- In Tom's Crafting Terminal the recipe viewer + button can craft missing ingredients (Ctrl + click).
