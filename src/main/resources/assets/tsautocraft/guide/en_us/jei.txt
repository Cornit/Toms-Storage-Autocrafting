JEI and REI

- Pattern Terminal: the + button fills the grid with the recipe (mode is chosen from the recipe type), or drag items onto grid slots.
- Pattern Provider: + encodes the recipe directly into a free slot, using a Blank Pattern from your inventory.
- Requester: + puts the recipe output into an entry; drag items onto entries.
- Exporter/Importer: drag items onto filter slots.
- Tom's Crafting Terminal: + with Ctrl + click crafts missing ingredients.
REI works like JEI (less tested).
