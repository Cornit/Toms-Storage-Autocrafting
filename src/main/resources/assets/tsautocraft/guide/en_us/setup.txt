Minimal setup

1. Build a Tom's storage: Inventory Connector next to chests, Inventory Cables from it.
2. Connect an Autocrafting Terminal (runs the jobs) and a Pattern Terminal to the cables.
3. Place a Pattern Provider on the cable network and a vanilla Crafter next to it.
4. Let the crafter drop its result into a chest and put an Importer (connected to the cables) on that chest, so results go back into storage.
5. The crafter needs a trigger: build your own redstone circuit, put a Redstone Upgrade into the provider, or set crafterTrigger to direct or redstone in the config.
6. Encode a crafting pattern in the Pattern Terminal and put it into the provider.
7. The item now shows a green + in every terminal. Ctrl + click it, enter an amount, Calculate, Start.
