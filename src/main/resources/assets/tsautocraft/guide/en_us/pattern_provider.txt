Pattern Provider

Holds patterns (9 slots, +9 per Capacity Upgrade up to 36) and delivers the inputs to machines when a job needs them.

- Full block: supplies every adjacent crafter or machine, one after the other. A redstone pulse only goes to the machine that was just filled.
- Connector (and framed connector): supplies only the block it faces.
- Crafting patterns fill a vanilla Crafter in pattern order. Processing patterns insert all inputs at once or nothing.
- Blocking mode: only push again once no inputs are left in the machine.
- Lock mode: off, until result returned (waits for the Importer), or with/without redstone signal.
- Priority: providers with a higher priority are used first.
- Results must come back through an Importer (or directly into storage).
- Upgrades: Capacity, Speed, Stack, Redstone.
